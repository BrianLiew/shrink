# Shrink Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

**Shrink collects no data.** It has no network access, analytics, tracking, advertising or accounts, it doesn't save your text or summaries, and nothing is shared with Shrink's developer or anyone else.

## Your text

When you summarize text, through Services, the keyboard shortcut or Safari, it's processed on your Mac by Apple Intelligence's on-device model. The app and its Safari extension have network access switched off, so Shrink can't send your text anywhere. (Copying is the one way text can reach your other devices; see [Clipboard](#clipboard).) A summary is gone once you close it. Apple's model is covered by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Safari extension

The extension needs website access so it can offer "Summarize with Shrink" when you right-click. On each right-click it reads the selected text, including text in a text box but never in a password field, and uses it only to show that menu item and, if you choose it, to summarize it.

## Clipboard

- The **Copy** buttons, and ⌘C in the summary panel with nothing selected, copy to this Mac only.
- If you select part of a summary and press ⌘C in the panel, macOS copies it normally for a moment before Shrink replaces it with a copy for this Mac only, so it may briefly sync to your other devices if Universal Clipboard is on.
- Copying selected text with right-click → Copy (in the panel or the Safari popover), or with ⌘C in the Safari popover, uses standard macOS copy, which may sync to your other devices if Universal Clipboard is on.

## Stored on your Mac

One setting: whether you've seen the setup guide. It contains no personal information. Shrink also checks, without saving anything, whether its "Summarize with Shrink" service and its Safari extension are switched on.

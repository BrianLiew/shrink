# Shrink Support

## Shortcut and Services menu

### The shortcut (⌃⇧⌘S) doesn't work, or "Summarize with Shrink" is missing from the Services menu

macOS keeps new services switched off. Open System Settings → Keyboard, click **Keyboard Shortcuts…**, select **Services** on the left, and under **Text** tick **Summarize with Shrink**.

Shrink's setup guide walks you through this: choose **How to Use Shrink…** from its menu bar icon, or open Shrink again.

### It works in some apps but not others

Not every app offers Services for its text. Copy the text into an app such as TextEdit and press ⌃⇧⌘S there, or use Safari's right-click item for web pages.

## Safari

### "Summarize with Shrink" doesn't appear when I right-click

- **Turn the extension on:** Safari → Settings → Extensions → tick **Shrink**.
- **Allow it on websites:** in the same pane, click **Edit Websites…** and allow it on other websites. Or wait for Safari to ask the first time you right-click, and choose **Always Allow on Every Website**.
- **Select some text first.** The item only appears when text is selected, and never in password fields.
- **Some web editors** (such as online document editors) draw their own selection and don't share it with extensions. Copy the text into another app and press ⌃⇧⌘S there instead.
- If it still doesn't appear on a page, try reloading the page.

### I choose "Summarize with Shrink", but nothing happens

The summary opens from Shrink's **Summarize** toolbar button, so the button needs to be in Safari's toolbar. Right-click the toolbar, choose **Customize Toolbar…**, and drag the button back in. Then right-click your selection and choose **Summarize with Shrink** again. Clicking the button on its own may show an older summary.

## Summaries

### Which language is the summary in?

The language of the text you selected, not your Mac's. If the text mixes languages, Shrink goes by the main one.

### I got a message from the model instead of a summary

Apple's model sometimes declines to summarize a passage and explains why. Shrink shows that reply as it is. Try a different selection.

## Messages

### "Turn on Apple Intelligence in System Settings to summarize text."

Shrink uses Apple Intelligence's on-device model. Open System Settings, find **Apple Intelligence**, and turn it on.

### "This device doesn't support Apple Intelligence."

Shrink needs a Mac that supports Apple Intelligence, so it can't run on this Mac.

### "The on-device model isn't ready yet. Try again in a little while."

Usually macOS is still downloading or setting up the model, for example just after you turned Apple Intelligence on. It can also appear when the model is busy or couldn't produce a summary for that text. Wait a minute and try again. If it keeps happening with one passage while other text works, Shrink can't summarize that passage.

### "This language isn't supported yet."

Shrink summarizes text in English, Danish, Dutch, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Norwegian, Portuguese, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, Swedish, Traditional Chinese, Turkish and Vietnamese.

### "Select a little more text to get a summary."

Shrink needs about a paragraph (roughly 100 words) to work with. Select more text.

### "That selection is too long to summarize."

Shrink handles up to roughly 18,000 words, fewer in Chinese, Japanese or Korean. Select a section and summarize it on its own.

## The app

### Where is Shrink? It has no Dock icon

Shrink lives in the menu bar. Its menu has **How to Use Shrink…** and **Quit Shrink**. If you can't see its icon (for example, when the menu bar is full), open Shrink from your Applications folder to show the guide.

### How do I uninstall Shrink?

Choose **Quit Shrink** from its menu bar icon, then move Shrink from your Applications folder to the Trash. Its Safari extension goes with it.
